What Pip does

A little help to get going, and a friend who cheers when you do.

  • Start the thing

    Tell Pip what you’ve been putting off, and Pip turns it into one small first step. Add habits and gentle reminders when you want them, and stop whenever you need to.

  • Pip goes on adventures

    Your real-life progress sends Pip off exploring. Pip comes home with items and pets to collect.

  • Make Pip yours

    Dress Pip up in hats, capes, wands and more. Unlock new looks as Pip levels up.

Tiny Pip never rewards screen time. Pip only earns from what you actually do, so real life always comes first.
