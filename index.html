How it works

One thing at a time. Real progress. A little adventure for Pip.

  1. Pick one thing

    Tell Pip what you’ve been putting off: a bill, a call, a messy room, a work task. Just one.

  2. Take a tiny first step

    Pip suggests a small first step to begin with. Tap “I’ve started,” and Pip cheers you on.

  3. Keep going, or stop

    Finish steps, log your habits, or run a short focus timer. When you’re done for now, that’s okay.

  4. Pip goes exploring

    Your real progress earns Pip time to explore. Pip comes home with items, pets and coins.

Questions

What is Tiny Pip?

Tiny Pip is a small companion app that helps with procrastination: the everyday tasks, habits and bigger responsibilities you keep putting off. Pip helps you pick one thing, start it with a tiny first step, and log your real progress. That progress sends Pip on adventures to find items and pets.

When is Tiny Pip coming out?

Tiny Pip is in beta now, on iPhone and Android. Join the beta to try it today. When you tap Get my rewards in the app, we’ll email you when Tiny Pip launches on the App Store and Google Play.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Tiny Pip is coming to both the App Store and Google Play, and the beta is on both.

How does the beta work?

On iPhone, you test through Apple’s TestFlight app. On Android, you join our Google Group, become a tester on Google Play, then install from Google Play. Then open Tiny Pip and tap Get my rewards: it asks for your email and links your tester rewards to the app, so there’s no code to type. The beta is free, you can leave anytime, and you can send feedback from inside the app to help shape what Pip does next.

Can I join the beta now?

Yes. Use the buttons above and you can start right away. There’s no invite to wait for.

What do I need for the beta?

An iPhone with Apple’s free TestFlight app, or an Android phone with Google Play.

What do beta testers get?

Testers who tap Get my rewards in the app and play on 5 different days get two thank-yous at launch: 3 months of Plus, free (no card and nothing to cancel; we give it to you ourselves, not through the App Store or Google Play), and the Founder’s Workshop gear, an outfit for Pip nobody can get later. If you reinstall or change phones, tap Get my rewards and add the same email again, and they come back.

What happens to the email I add for my rewards?

We keep it with your tester code and use it only to give you your tester rewards and to tell you when Tiny Pip launches. It’s never sold or shared, and we delete it within 90 days after launch. Our privacy policy has the details.

Is Tiny Pip free?

Yes, Tiny Pip is free to download and use. An optional Plus plan, with extra places to explore and a few extras, is coming at launch. Starting tasks, reminders, how much you earn and the odds of a find are the same for everyone, and anything you earn is yours to keep. Free and Plus has the details.

Does Pip make me feel bad if I miss a day?

Never. There are no streaks to lose. Missing a day doesn’t reset anything, each week starts fresh, and when you come back, Pip is just happy to see you. More on why Tiny Pip is built to be gentle.

Can I use it for work tasks?

Yes. Personal or work, big or small: anything you’ve been putting off. Bigger tasks can be split into steps, and Pip remembers where you left off. See how breaking tasks down works.

Is Tiny Pip for people with ADHD?

Tiny Pip is for anyone who knows exactly what to do and still can’t start it. A lot of people with ADHD know that feeling well, and Pip was made for that moment: one thing, one tiny first step, and no guilt if you stop. It isn’t a treatment for ADHD or anything else, just a little help getting started. More about Tiny Pip and ADHD.

Does the game reward screen time?

No. Pip only earns from what you actually do: starting, finishing and logging real progress. Time spent in the app earns nothing, and Pip will happily suggest a break.

Is my information private?

Yes. The app has no account, and what you put into Tiny Pip stays on your phone, mood check-ins included. There are two optional exceptions. If you tap “Help me break this down,” just that one task’s words are sent to be turned into steps, and we don’t keep them. And if you tap Get my rewards in the app, the app’s random ID is linked to the email you add, only so we can give you your tester rewards. Your beta stats are never linked to your email. See our privacy policy for the details.

Is Tiny Pip a mental health app?

No. Tiny Pip is an everyday companion for tasks and habits, not therapy or medical care. If you’re struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a local support line.
