How it works

One thing at a time. Real progress. A little adventure for Pip.

  1. Pick one thing

    Tell Pip what you’ve been putting off: a bill, a call, a messy room, a work task. Just one.

  2. Take a tiny first step

    Pip suggests a small first step to begin with. Tap “I’ve started,” and Pip cheers you on.

  3. Keep going, or stop

    Finish steps, log your habits, or run a short focus timer. When you’re done for now, that’s okay.

  4. Pip goes exploring

    Your real progress earns Pip time to explore. Pip comes home with items, pets and coins.

Questions

What is Tiny Pip?

Tiny Pip is a small companion app for the things you put off: everyday tasks, habits and bigger responsibilities. Pip helps you pick one thing, start it with a tiny first step, and log your real progress. That progress sends Pip on adventures to find items and pets.

When is Tiny Pip coming out?

Tiny Pip is in a small beta now, and we’re opening it to more testers soon. Join the beta and we’ll email you an invite when there’s a spot, and again when Tiny Pip launches on the App Store and Google Play.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Tiny Pip is coming to both the App Store and Google Play. When you sign up for the beta, tell us which phone you use so we can send you the right invite.

How does the beta work?

Sign up with your email and choose iPhone or Android. When a spot opens, we’ll email you an invite: on iPhone, you’ll test through Apple’s TestFlight app, and on Android, through Google Play. The beta is free, you can leave anytime, and your feedback helps shape Tiny Pip.

Is Tiny Pip free?

Yes, Tiny Pip will be free to download and use. We may add an optional paid plan later with extra content, but starting tasks, reminders and everything you earn will always be free.

Does Pip make me feel bad if I miss a day?

Never. There are no streaks to lose. Missing a day doesn’t reset anything, each week starts fresh, and when you come back, Pip is just happy to see you.

Can I use it for work tasks?

Yes. Personal or work, big or small: anything you’ve been putting off. Bigger tasks can be split into steps, and Pip remembers where you left off.

Does the game reward screen time?

No. Pip only earns from what you actually do: starting, finishing and logging real progress. Time spent in the app earns nothing, and Pip will happily suggest a break.

Is my information private?

Yes. There’s no account or sign-up, and what you put into Tiny Pip stays on your phone, mood check-ins included. The only exception is optional: if you tap “Help me break this down,” just that one task’s words are sent to be turned into steps, and we don’t keep them. See our privacy policy for the details.

Is Tiny Pip a mental health app?

No. Tiny Pip is an everyday companion for tasks and habits, not therapy or medical care. If you’re struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a local support line.
