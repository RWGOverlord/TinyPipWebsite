What Tiny Pip does

What Tiny Pip does, at a glance

Most to-do apps help you remember what to do. Tiny Pip is for when you already know, and still can’t begin. It helps you pick one thing, find a first step small enough to take, and feel good about taking it. Your real progress then sends Pip, your small companion, off on adventures.

In short: Tiny Pip is a gentle productivity and self-care companion. It turns the thing you keep putting off into one tiny first step, rewards you for starting, not just finishing, and never punishes you for missing a day.

How Tiny Pip treats you

  • Less time in the app, not more

    Tiny Pip never rewards screen time. Opening the app earns nothing, Pip explores while the app is closed, and the point of every screen is to get you back to your real day.

  • No account, and it stays on your phone

    There’s nothing to sign up for. Your tasks, habits and check-ins are saved on your phone. The one exception is the optional AI helper, which sends just that one task’s words. Privacy policy.

AI help

What the AI does, and what it doesn’t

Tiny Pip uses AI for one thing: when you tap Help me break this down, it suggests small steps for that task. Everything else, including Pip’s ready-made steps, reminders, check-ins and the game, works on your phone without AI.

  • It only runs when you ask, one task at a time.
  • It suggests steps; you decide which ones to keep.
  • It isn’t a coach, a counselor or a therapist, and it doesn’t chat with you about your feelings.
