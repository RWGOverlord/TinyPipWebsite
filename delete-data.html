Tiny Pip

Delete your Tiny Pip data

Tiny Pip is an app by Emberforge Works, LLC. It keeps almost everything on your phone, so most of it is yours to delete right away. Here’s how to delete each part.

1. What’s on your phone

Your tasks, steps, habits, reminders, moods, answers to Pip’s questions, game progress and settings.

Uninstall Tiny Pip. Everything it stored on your phone is deleted right away. If your phone backs up apps (to your Google account or iCloud), also delete Tiny Pip’s backup in your phone’s backup settings. If you saved a copy with Save my data, delete that file wherever you put it.

2. The AI helper’s records

If you used “Help me break this down”, our server keeps a random ID for your phone, when you asked, whether it worked, and what it cost. Never the words of your tasks.

Email feedback@heytinypip.com and ask us to delete your AI helper data. We delete it within 30 days.

3. Beta stats and questions (test versions only)

If you said yes to sharing with the team during the beta (game numbers, usage counts, answers to Pip’s questions and any notes you sent).

In the app: open Settings → Beta → Delete my beta data. Everything sent from your phone is deleted at once, and sharing turns off. Your tasks, habits and game progress stay.

Or by email: find your beta ID under Settings → Beta and email it to feedback@heytinypip.com; we delete it within 30 days. Turning sharing off stops anything more being sent. All beta data is deleted within 90 days after Tiny Pip’s public launch anyway.

4. Feedback emails and the waitlist

Email us and we’ll delete your feedback emails, and your waitlist details if you joined it. You can also leave the waitlist any time with the unsubscribe link in our emails.

What we keep

After a deletion request, nothing tied to you: only totals that can’t be traced back to anyone, such as how many AI requests were made in a month.
